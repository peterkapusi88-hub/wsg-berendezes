## 1. DNS és WOW-próba
A Japanese collector's apartment drawn with one confident ink stroke: graphite, smoke-grey paper fibre and taupe weave. Broad unequal planes, disciplined joints and a single oversized mark create quiet tension; the room feels commissioned, tactile and inhabited.

A három látványhordozó: a saját TV-fal tömeg/árnyék ritmusa; W02+W07 nagy léptékű összefüggő kompozíciója; az önálló master fejvég és gardrób. A karakter nappali fényben is megmarad, nem csak meleg színkorrekciótól működik.

Use graphite compact laminate with a smoke-grey surface and a narrow black recessed pull. The basin is visually nested into the continuous counter, with a legible rim and sealed perimeter. Retain the current 60 × 48 cm Geberit Smyle basin only if its manufacturer approves this installation; otherwise show the integrated-basin design intent as conditional on an approved inset replacement at the existing connections. LAYERED LIGHT. a shadow slot behind the mirror washes the grey tile like a single brushstroke; a separate recessed line under the vanity produces a quiet floating edge. Use two separately controllable Philips Hue Lightstrip Plus V4 2 m kits as the electrical basis: one rear-mirror route, one recessed under-vanity route. The final lit lengths follow approved cut marks and the surveyed cabinet width. Both are hidden in bathroom-suitable sealed aluminium profiles with opal covers and sealed cable entries; no bare LEDs appear in glass or mirror reflections. Keep existing ceiling and basin points for conditional Hue Adore-family general and facial lighting. GLASS AND REFLECTION DETAIL. Render the screen as transparent glass with restrained edge reflections and believable water separation, showing the vanity next to its dry-side edge. Keep the entry passage and actual shower depth. The mirror reflects only the real opposite wall or door according to the fixed camera. The cabinet glow has a physical source, a soft cutoff and a restrained falloff across the floor. Artificial light is the only illumination; the glass does not become an invented window.
