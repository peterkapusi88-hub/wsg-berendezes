## 1. DNS és WOW-próba
A personal collector's home with olive upholstery, a monumental original plum-and-chalk artwork, walnut-effect storage and smoky woven walls. The atmosphere comes from a few meaningful objects and deliberate asymmetry, not a crowded gallery or borrowed historic mouldings.

A három látványhordozó: a saját TV-fal tömeg/árnyék ritmusa; W02+W07 nagy léptékű összefüggő kompozíciója; az önálló master fejvég és gardrób. A karakter nappali fényben is megmarad, nem csak meleg színkorrekciótól működik.

## 4. Fürdő 06.2 – új tulajdonosi irány
Keep the real windowless L-shaped bathroom 06.2, existing Geostone Grigio 60 × 60 tile, Geo Esagonetta Grigio 3D relief, black Grohe fittings and sanitary connection positions. Add a fixed clear safety-glass shower screen with a 100 cm horizontal length along the existing open shower edge. Its proposed 200 cm height and nominal 8 mm glass thickness are design intentions for the glass specialist to verify. The entry remains open and usable. Keep the original shower floor, fall and drain, with a sealed screen foot and discreet black wall channel. The glass has its own rated support; neither the vanity nor the heating/cooling ceiling supports it.

INTEGRATED VANITY. Run one continuous made-to-measure basin cabinet from the new glass boundary to the actual adjacent wall, with a hygienically sealed movement joint beside the glass. The full width is the surveyed glass-to-wall distance, not an invented number. Propose a 50 cm depth, 85 cm finished counter height and 35 cm cabinet-body height for testing against the real basin, pipework and walking space. Use smoke-grey waterproof compact laminate with an ivory counter and a dark narrow pull line. The basin is visually nested into the continuous counter, with a legible rim and sealed perimeter. Retain the current 60 × 48 cm Geberit Smyle basin only if its manufacturer approves this installation; otherwise show the integrated-basin design intent as conditional on an approved inset replacement at the existing connections. Ceramic is never cut. Provide a removable siphon back and working drawers around the plumbing. The prior separate 55 cm under-basin box is replaced by this single continuous composition.

WALL STORAGE AND ART DIRECTION. Use an ivory-surrounded mirror and a smoke-grey closed side cabinet, 22 cm wide, 75 cm high and 12 cm deep. The cabinet is on the dry side outside the shower and mirror sightline, with real door clearance, concealed rated wall fixings and a washable lower shelf. Its dimensions are proposed envelopes. Preserve the actual tile behind it and radiator access. Style with a small plum glazed vessel and an ivory tray with one neatly folded towel. Let the objects feel selected and useful; their scale leaves the basin usable. The room gains character from the mirror outline, cabinet proportions and reflected light rather than added architectural niches.

LAYERED LIGHT. a softly concealed upper-and-side mirror wash resembles gallery illumination; an understated underside line gives depth. Use two separately controllable Philips Hue Lightstrip Plus V4 2 m kits as the electrical basis: one rear-mirror route, one recessed under-vanity route. The final lit lengths follow approved cut marks and the surveyed cabinet width. Both are hidden in bathroom-suitable sealed aluminium profiles with opal covers and sealed cable entries; no bare LEDs appear in glass or mirror reflections. Feed them only from the existing above-basin circuit through a locally compliant, accessible dry-side conversion and service enclosure, if a qualified electrician approves the wet-zone classification, permanent Hue supply and protection. Do not invent a bathroom socket. The two supplies remain ventilated and accessible outside prohibited zones. Keep existing ceiling and basin points for conditional Hue Adore-family general and facial lighting. Use 2700 K for the concealed ambient layers and 3500 K for facial/general light, with moderate exposure and naturally readable grey tiles. If the strip supply or zone cannot be approved, use a certified Hue bathroom fitting at the existing basin point to create the wall wash; retain the same furniture and mirror silhouette.

GLASS AND REFLECTION DETAIL. Render the screen as transparent glass with restrained edge reflections and believable water separation, showing the vanity next to its dry-side edge. Keep the entry passage and actual shower depth. The mirror reflects only the real opposite wall or door according to the fixed camera. The cabinet glow has a physical source, a soft cutoff and a restrained falloff across the floor. Artificial light is the only illumination; the glass does not become an invented window.

A korábbi üveg nélküli és 55 cm különálló mosdóbútor-változatot ez felváltja. A mosdószekrény pontos szélessége helyszíni mérésig ismeretlen; az üveg 100 cm hossza új tulajdonosi kérés. A süllyesztett beépítéshez szükséges mosdócsere feltételes, 100 eFt előirányzattal.
