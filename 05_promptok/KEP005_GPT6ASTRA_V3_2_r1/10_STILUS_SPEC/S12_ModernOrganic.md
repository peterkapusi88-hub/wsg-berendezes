## 1. DNS és WOW
A sculptural penthouse in warm chalk, mushroom, dark cocoa and soft flax. The owner's IMG_2589 montage contributes the tension between rough mineral surfaces, pillowy pale upholstery and a few dark reflective objects; the Etsy relief contributes the scale of shadow within a pale monochrome field. The original signature, TIDAL SHOULDER, is a broad asymmetric folded ridge with a low hollow, deliberately distinct from the reference artwork. Large quiet masses and real tactile shadows produce luxury within the existing apartment shell.
