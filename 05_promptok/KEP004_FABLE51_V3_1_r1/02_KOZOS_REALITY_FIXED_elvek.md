# Common REALITY / FIXED principles – B contestant (Claude Fable 5.1), V3.1 r1

Source: prompt template v3.1 §1 (geometry and camera, existing surfaces, honest material names, circulation) – adopted; **not** adopted: "Walls and ceilings: painted white" (replaced by each style's wall plan; the ceiling stays white) and the "Series A neutral overcast" light rule (replaced by VB.3 per-camera light). Camera numbers are the common VB.3 data; Claude adds the final FIXED / FRAME LAYOUT block from Blender. These principles are written into every one of the 60 prompts in condensed, view-specific form; this file is the reference wording.

## R1 · Geometry and camera (image 1 is authoritative)
Use the neutral Blender base image of the named camera strictly for room geometry, openings, floor–wall lines and camera. Keep the exact framing and the 2.90 m white ceiling. Do not widen the room, do not add or move walls, pillars, niches, bulkheads, coves or ceiling fixtures. The ceiling carries heating/cooling panels: nothing is attached to it except the existing light points (living ML04, ML05; master ML06; bathroom ML10). Vertical lines stay vertical.

## R2 · Existing surfaces that never change
- **Floor** in living, dining, kitchen, corridor and bedrooms: the existing medium grey-brown oak laminate with visible grain and knots (plan: Egger grey oak; exact decor NOT VERIFIED on site) with 6 cm matching skirting – not light oak, not cool grey, not concrete, not stone.
- **Ceiling** white, smooth, no colour cast; **walls** white wherever a style names no treatment.
- **Windows and terrace glazing**: white frames inside, anthracite outside, set in a dark brick reveal; the glass division as in image 1; exterior roller shutters up, their service cover free.
- **Interior doors** light grey with black handles (the 05.3 door on W03 at y 6.05–6.95; the master door; the bathroom door): colour, position, frame and handle unchanged.
- **Switches, thermostat (T01), sensor (S01), intercom, consumer unit** stay visible and free: living switch column at the east end of W02 (K10–K12, D19, T01, S01), dining K07–K09 + D12 beside the fridge, corridor K25–K27 + D46, master K13/K14 and D20–D23 beside the bed.
- **Terrace**: ceramic tiles −0.15 m, raised ribbed brown WPC deck +0.62 m, green roof, anthracite railing, real Buda hillside, church spire and city roofs – no other skyline, water or greenery.
- **Bathroom 06.2**: Ceramiche Piemme Geostone Grigio matt 60×60 porcelain on floor and walls to 2.85 m, Geo Esagonetta Grigio 3D decor on the shower back wall and above the WC ledge, walk-in shower without glass screen, 70 cm linear drain, black Grohe fittings, white Geberit Smyle Square basin and WC, black towel radiator, no window – nothing re-tiled, moved or added except mirror, storage, light fittings on the existing points, textiles and accessories.

## R3 · Honest material names
Laminate reads as laminate (Egger H1180 Halifax oak natural, H3710 Carini walnut, F274 light concrete families, U-codes for unicolours), MDF as painted or printed MDF, bent MDF as bent MDF, veneer only on the one surface the style names, vinyl-backed textile wallcovering as wallcovering, printed canvas as printed canvas, upholstered panel as upholstered panel, stone-effect porcelain or panel as stone-effect (never "travertine" or "marble"). Real metal, glass, ceramics and natural stone only where a verified product says so (e.g. a small real-marble side-table top). No solid timber fronts, no real stone cladding, no painted fronts unless the style says so with owner approval (BiBo: navy laminate base). Cabinet interiors are white.

## R4 · Products and dimensions
Only the products and envelopes named in the style's list, at their real dimensions and handedness; no enlarging, no shrinking to fit, no extra large furniture. Heights of built elements are given as numbers in each prompt and must be kept (the V3 test images were taller than specified). A piece whose dimension is unknown is described without a number, never with an invented one.

## R5 · Circulation (checked later by Claude in Blender)
Main routes ≥ 0.90 m with chairs pushed in (terrace sliding door, kitchen, corridor to the bedrooms, 05.3 door); chair pull-out ≥ 0.90 m; nearest sofa point to TV-wall furniture ≥ 0.60 m; sofa to coffee table 0.45–0.50 m; ≥ 0.60 m beside the bed; doors swing free; the Cam_12 standing spot (x 4.30–4.50, y 7.75–8.05) stays empty.

## R6 · Layout C (living–dining–kitchen) – common base
Sofa minimum L, preferably U, its back to the east facing the dining zone, the long seat north–south, open to the west TV wall; the north end leaves the glass free. Dining: a 160 × 90 cm envelope under the pendant point ML04, six seats 2+2+1+1 (unless a style states a tested alternative and its compromise). Kitchen: IKEA METOD carcasses in two runs 62 cm deep (clear width 85–86 cm, CONDITIONAL), the ~0.85 m west parapet kept (no island, no full-height wall), fridge column at D13 in front of W03-a, fronts/worktop/splashback per style, worktop light only from FL01 and ML03, pendant only at ML04.

## R7 · Light – VB.3 per camera (identical for all ten styles)
- **Cam_02 living–terrace**: blue hour / early evening. Exterior sky deep blue, city lights beginning; interior lit only by the named Hue layers at 2200–2700 K, no RGB colour. Medium exposure: no crushed blacks, no blown highlights; wall edges, openings, floor–wall line, terrace tiles, railing and green roof stay readable. Hidden LED lines glow evenly from profiles with opal diffusers – never visible LED dots. Light only from the fittings named in the prompt.
- **Cam_12 living hero**: daytime, bright natural luxury-interior photography; soft north daylight through the glass behind the camera; the Hue scene off or barely perceptible (a pendant at ML04 may be on at low level). The TV wall and the terrace are not in frame.
- **Cam_03 kitchen–dining**: daytime, natural light; worktop light from FL01 restrained.
- **Cam_04 master**: early evening Hue scene – headboard light, reading lights, the ML06 fitting at low level; outside the glass is dusk-blue.
- **Cam_11 master panorama**: daytime, neutral daylight, the real view; fittings off.
- **Cam_10 bathroom 06.2**: artificial light only, from ML10 (ceiling) and FL04 (above the basin), 2700–3000 K, no invented window or skylight.

## R8 · Hue system rule (VB.7)
All general and decorative light is Philips Hue: E27/E14/GU10 fittings that physically take a Hue bulb, Hue-made fittings, or Hue Lightstrip Plus / Gradient in aluminium profiles with opal covers. Strip power from an existing socket (D-ID) via a hidden driver; where the cable route is not verified the element is "CONDITIONAL – CLAUDE ELECTRICAL AUDIT". Cabinet interiors may use non-Hue functional light. No ceiling drilling, no new outlets.

## R9 · Cross-view identity
Cam_02, Cam_12 and Cam_03 show the one living–dining–kitchen plan of the style (same sofa, table, chairs, pendant, wall treatments, kitchen fronts, rug, curtains); Cam_04 and Cam_11 show the one master plan (bed 180×200, axis y 6.40, headboard composition, north niche wardrobe wall 3.00 × 0.90 × 2.90 m flush with the pillar, south 2.42 m wardrobe strip in the same front family). The same product has the same name, size and colour in every view.

## R10 · Prohibitions used in prompts (max ~8 per image, only proven failures)
Physically impossible mirror reflections; light brick reveal instead of the real dark one; a floor lighter or cooler than the real laminate; a different number of fronts than specified; a floor-to-ceiling mirror; furniture taller or shifted from the specified height/position; invented ceiling light points, coves, bulkheads or skylights; a different panorama.
