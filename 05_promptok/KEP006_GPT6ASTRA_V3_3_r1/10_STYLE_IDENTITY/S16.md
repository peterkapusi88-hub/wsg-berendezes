## DNS és színpaletta

PRECISION SOFT translates the owner’s own G 63 into a warm home: softly squared ivory upholstery held inside satin graphite planes, fine silver-grey metal edges and restrained grey timber. The exterior photographs suggest a low-gloss mineral grey with changing warm and cool reflections; the cabin photographs show cream upholstery, narrow stitched channels, perforation, dark upper surfaces and silver details. These are visual observations, not verified paint or leather codes. The owner-supplied Monza Grey magno name remains an inspiration pending factory documentation. Form is substantial and protective, while the inhabited surfaces are soft and luminous.
