# S15 · Authentic Japanese Modern · STYLE IDENTITY SHEET V3.3

Státusz: kreatív terv; katalógus-, geometriai és szakági kapuk külön.

## DNS és színpaletta

A contemporary Japanese-informed home composed through intervals, asymmetry and carefully framed everyday use. Ash-grey wood, smoke-brown laminate, charcoal, off-white cloth and filtered light create a slightly darker atmosphere without red or orange timber. Empty surfaces are active parts of the design. The original signature, STILL INTERVAL, is a shallow pale folded plane beside a quiet dark void; its identity lies in proportion and material, not calligraphy, symbols or themed decoration.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

STILL WALL: a 360 × 288 × 3.6 cm applied field alternates one broad ash-grey wood-effect panel with a quiet charcoal screen plane. A 240 × 42 × 40 cm low cabinet in neutral smoke-brown laminate has four plain working doors, recessed dark pulls and a grounded 6 cm shadow plinth. At the south end, a 65 × 245 × 40 cm closed tower uses two unequal ash-grey doors. Between tower and screen, retain a 16 cm-wide dark empty interval; this is a recessed furniture joint, not a new wall opening. A small pale horizontal display ledge within the tower's fixed end holds a single charcoal vessel, behind the cabinet's external depth envelope. The TV occupies its clear 190 cm bay and sits on an independent rear-wall bracket. The wall's distinction comes from unequal calm fields, true grain and one inhabited void.

## Integrált főmű

A full-height off-white paper-fibre field meets one asymmetrical charcoal interval and a shallow horizontal fold at hand height. Its outer pale edge fades into ash-grey wallcovering, while the dark interval remains a genuine panel gap with a closed backing. Keep the surrounding wall calm and unlettered. This is contemporary measured craft, not calligraphy, a temple screen or a faux tatami room. W03, W04 and W07 retain the established style ground, actual grey doors and white window frames. Keep switches, thermostat, sensors and service points visible and operable. All panel dimensions follow surveyed wall extents, not image-derived dimensions.

## Konyha

Neutral ash-grey wood-effect lower fronts, charcoal tall fronts and plain off-white upper cabinets use a simple functional METOD grid. A grey fine-grain laminate worktop and matching smooth backsplash keep the preparation area calm. A single horizontal dark pull line unites the lower doors; the D13 fridge tower and real csonk remain distinct functional volumes. The wood sample is explicitly neutral grey-brown with no red, orange, cherry or teak cast. One low black ceramic bowl is useful rather than ornamental.

## Kisasztal, textilek, tárgyak

A 70 × 35 × 45 cm ash-grey laminate coffee block has flat planes, a dark recessed base and one gently softened exposed edge. Its 30 × 45 × 30 cm smoke-brown companion carries a single handmade charcoal tea cup. Chalk and ash-grey cushions and one fine graphite throw soften the taupe sofa. Full-height off-white sheers filter the real glazing, with smoke-grey heavier curtains stacking clear of the sash. A single dark ceramic bowl and two closed books leave the table mostly empty. The oak dining table's natural light veneer remains its actual material; the strongly visible custom timber planes are all neutral ash-grey or smoke-brown, never reddish.

## Master és gardrób

A 270 × 145 × 7 cm headboard has a broad ash-grey woven upholstered centre within a smoke-brown laminate frame. The upper line is slightly offset from the two bedside blocks, creating a calm asymmetrical interval rather than a decorative screen. The existing white wall above remains quiet. North wardrobe fronts fill the niche with six ash-grey wood-effect doors, dark recessed pulls and fitted upper infill; south fronts repeat the same neutral grain and working joints. Off-white bedding, a charcoal fine-weave quilt and one ash-grey cushion have natural folds. A small pale folded-paper object in a shallow bedside tray echoes STILL INTERVAL once. The ordinary bed height and useful storage remain; no raised tatami platform alters the floor or passage.

## Terasz

A 70 × 45 cm graphite outdoor table carries one pale serving bowl. A separate 140 × 80 cm charcoal outdoor dining table and four slim chairs occupy a measured lower paved bay. These budgeted procurement envelopes require product selection. Preserve the existing dark-brown WPC as an existing material rather than recolouring it to match new ash-grey furniture. Keep every step, the planted roof, railings and real city panorama. Two rechargeable outdoor-rated lanterns provide modest table-level light; the view and changing daylight remain the terrace's main event.
