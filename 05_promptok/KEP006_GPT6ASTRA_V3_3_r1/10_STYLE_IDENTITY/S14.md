# S14 · Modern White Luxury · STYLE IDENTITY SHEET V3.3

Státusz: kreatív terv; katalógus-, geometriai és szakági kapuk külön.

## DNS és színpaletta

A luminous collector's interior where the original white walls are valuable negative space. Chalk, oyster, pearl and soft grey separate through weave, gloss and shadow rather than a colour filter. A few confident furniture forms and one large physical artwork carry the room. The signature, WHITE ECLIPSE, is a pair of overlapping pale planes with a displaced charcoal edge: precise, tactile and quietly dramatic. Money saved on extensive cladding supports upholstery, artwork and the quality of light.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

WHITE ECLIPSE WALL: retain the original painted white wall across the full 360 cm design field. Against it, a 245 × 44 × 40 cm pearl laminate cabinet with four flat doors and a recessed charcoal plinth floats beneath a separate 190 × 120 × 2.5 cm oyster screen backboard. Shift the backboard slightly south relative to the lower cabinet, keeping the actual TV centred within its 190 cm bay and independently bracketed. One 65 × 245 × 40 cm pearl closed tower sits at the south end, its fixed outside cheek softly rounded while the doors remain flat. A narrow charcoal reveal separates the tower from the white wall. The composition has three true depths, so clean daylight creates the drama; the white background is neither covered with imitation marble nor treated as an unfinished blank.

## Integrált főmű

A floor-to-ceiling chalk field on W02 overlaps one oyster plane with a displaced charcoal reverse edge. A broad partial circular opening belongs only to the thin applied front layer, not the wall. Preserve generous uncoated white wall on the return; keep total depth at most 3 cm. The work’s absent frame and near-white material differences are its luxury, not marble print or extra gold. W03, W04 and W07 retain the established style ground, actual grey doors and white window frames. Keep switches, thermostat, sensors and service points visible and operable. All panel dimensions follow surveyed wall extents, not image-derived dimensions.

## Konyha

Oyster-white flat laminate fronts, white carcasses, fine graphite recessed pulls and a pale-grey fine-grain laminate worktop make a refined functional kitchen. The matching backsplash has a low sheen that catches window light differently from the matte fronts. One broad oyster refrigerator side ends the composition at the real csonk. Crisp sealed joints and well-proportioned door divisions provide luxury without extra wall cladding. A single pearl ceramic bowl remains clear of preparation space.

## Kisasztal, textilek, tárgyak

Layer a large chalk linen cushion, one oyster bouclé cushion and a narrow graphite-grey lumbar cushion against the taupe sofa. Keep broad areas of the original white wall visibly empty so the art and upholstery can dominate.

## Master és gardrób

A 270 × 155 × 7 cm oyster upholstered headboard has one broad raised padded plane and a thin pearl surround, with an offset vertical seam and a charcoal lower reveal. The original white wall above remains uncovered. North and south wardrobes are complete floor-to-ceiling pearl laminate compositions with real six-door north divisions, understated graphite finger pulls and fitted upper infills. Chalk bedding has an oyster coverlet and one graphite-grey fine border; the curtains are pale grey and oyster. A small white ceramic disc on one bedside block is the only echo of WHITE ECLIPSE. This room feels luxurious through generous textile scale and exact joinery, not more decoration.
