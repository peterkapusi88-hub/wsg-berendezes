# S06 · Zenit Atelier · STYLE IDENTITY SHEET V3.3

Státusz: kreatív terv; katalógus-, geometriai és szakági kapuk külön.

## DNS és színpaletta

A private atelier with smoked walnut-effect mass, graphite textile and a single luminous incision. Warmth comes from deep material layering and restrained olive and mustard textiles; the composition is more architectural and directional than the common Zenit calibration.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

ATELIER INCISION: a 340 × 253 × 3.8 cm graphite textile-effect screen backdrop meets a 90 × 250 × 40 cm smoked-walnut-effect cabinet. A recessed 12 cm-wide vertical display slit is formed entirely within the cabinet envelope and lined with dark laminate. A 240 × 48 × 40 cm low cabinet runs below the 190 cm clear screen field, its three broad fronts aligned with the tall cabinet. The 3 m light assembly follows the reveal and a short upper return; the surrounding joinery stays dark enough to reveal the cut without losing surface detail.

## Integrált főmű

A diagonal graphite incision crosses a full-height warm-grey mineral-print field and stops at one olive-grey low rectangle. Match the surrounding wall texture so the work emerges from the wall. A narrow physical shadow seam gives depth; retain the original TV incision as the room’s material counterpoint, keeping this W02 artwork low in contrast rather than making two competing dark walls. W03, W04 and W07 retain the established style ground, actual grey doors and white window frames. Keep switches, thermostat, sensors and service points visible and operable. All panel dimensions follow surveyed wall extents, not image-derived dimensions.

## Konyha

Smoked-walnut-effect H3710-family lower fronts and graphite upper fronts are balanced by a warm light-grey concrete-effect laminate worktop and backsplash. The refrigerator tower repeats walnut-effect vertical grain, while the south tall wall is graphite. Handles and appliance reveals align as a disciplined cabinet grid.

## Bútorazonosság

Westwing Lennon right-hand L, taupe woven upholstery, 238 × 180 × 68 cm; hat Kave Home Eamy, brown polyurethane, walnut-finish ash veneer and black metal; Kave Home Malenka brown wool, 200 × 300 cm; ML04: Kave Home Ardali Ø50 cm, smoked glass and brushed steel pendant.

## Kisasztal, textilek, tárgyak

Use the taupe Lennon in place of the earlier oversized Blok: the smoky cabinetry and incision carry the atelier identity. Brown wool underfoot, one mustard and two charcoal cushions, a broad taupe throw, two monographs and one dark ceramic bowl make the seating feel occupied. A 70 × 35 × 45 cm smoked-walnut-effect coffee block and a compact matching side block remain low.

## Master és gardrób

Three broad pearl textile panels form a 270 × 155 × 7 cm headboard, enclosed by a thin graphite perimeter and one asymmetric walnut-effect side return. Bedding combines chalk cotton, a taupe coverlet and a narrow olive throw. Six north wardrobe fronts use smoked-walnut-effect laminate with large uninterrupted grain fields; the south wardrobe uses the same vertical grain and slim recessed pulls.

## Terasz

A charcoal aluminium outdoor two-seat sofa, target 160 × 80 cm, and two 70 × 75 cm chairs use ash-grey cushions and low, simple profiles. A 70 × 45 cm graphite outdoor table carries one pale serving bowl. A separate 140 × 80 cm charcoal outdoor dining table and four slim chairs occupy a measured lower paved bay. These budgeted procurement envelopes require product selection. Preserve the existing dark-brown WPC as an existing material rather than recolouring it to match new ash-grey furniture. Keep every step, the planted roof, railings and real city panorama. Two rechargeable outdoor-rated lanterns provide modest table-level light; the view and changing daylight remain the terrace's main event.
