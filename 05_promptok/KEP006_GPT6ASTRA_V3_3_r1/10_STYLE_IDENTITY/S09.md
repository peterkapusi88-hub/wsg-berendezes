# S09 · Paris Collector · STYLE IDENTITY SHEET V3.3

Státusz: kreatív terv; katalógus-, geometriai és szakági kapuk külön.

## DNS és színpaletta

A personal collector's home with olive upholstery, a monumental original plum-and-chalk artwork, walnut-effect storage and smoky woven walls. The atmosphere comes from a few meaningful objects and deliberate asymmetry, not a crowded gallery or borrowed historic mouldings.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

COLLECTOR CABINET: a 240 × 48 × 38 cm walnut-effect lower cabinet contains one 45 cm-wide open book compartment, balanced by a 70 × 220 × 38 cm south cupboard. A 350 × 253 × 3.5 cm smoky textile-effect backdrop integrates the screen within a 190 cm clear rear-plane field. One thin dark upper frame connects the cupboard to a small display niche; books and a single object supply depth without a full library wall.

## Integrált főmű

A smoke-plum circular fragment is partially absorbed by a full-height ivory woven field, with a charcoal low band linking it to the library base. Keep a deliberate unprinted margin at the adjacent return, not a thin picture frame. Preserve the library TV identity. A single small bedside work may remain as an intimate collector object only if it is visually subordinate to the upholstered headboard, never enlarged into a competing mural. W03, W04 and W07 retain the established style ground, actual grey doors and white window frames. Keep switches, thermostat, sensors and service points visible and operable. All panel dimensions follow surveyed wall extents, not image-derived dimensions.

## Konyha

Walnut-effect lower fronts, smoky-grey upper laminate and a restrained charcoal laminate worktop with grey washable backsplash create a quiet background for the collected art. A walnut-effect refrigerator flank aligns with the dining chairs; flat fronts and dark small pulls keep the kitchen from becoming period furniture.

## Bútorazonosság

Westwing Melva left-hand L, olive fine-woven upholstery, 238 × 143 × 75 cm; hat Kave Home Eamy, brown polyurethane, walnut-finish ash veneer and black metal; Kave Home Malenka brown wool, 200 × 300 cm; ML04: Kave Home Ardali Ø50 cm, smoked glass and brushed steel pendant.

## Kisasztal, textilek, tárgyak

The olive L, brown wool rug, two chalk linen cushions and a smoky plum cushion build a collected rather than matched setting. A 70 × 35 × 45 cm walnut-effect coffee block holds two monographs and a small glass vessel; one 30 × 45 × 30 cm side block remains clear for a cup. The earlier Corvena armchair is excluded from the base composition until a full-size circulation test proves it fits.

## Master és gardrób

A 270 × 150 × 6 cm smoky-plum textile headboard is bordered by a thin charcoal line and a pale vertical linen strip on one side. Chalk cotton bedding and a soft smoky-plum coverlet are paired with a warm grey throw. Six pale-grey north wardrobe fronts use a narrow dark inset printed perimeter; the south wardrobe follows the same family. A small original line print rests on one bedside block, mechanically secured.

## Terasz

A charcoal aluminium outdoor two-seat sofa, target 160 × 80 cm, and two 70 × 75 cm chairs use ash-grey cushions and low, simple profiles. A 70 × 45 cm graphite outdoor table carries one pale serving bowl. A separate 140 × 80 cm charcoal outdoor dining table and four slim chairs occupy a measured lower paved bay. These budgeted procurement envelopes require product selection. Preserve the existing dark-brown WPC as an existing material rather than recolouring it to match new ash-grey furniture. Keep every step, the planted roof, railings and real city panorama. Two rechargeable outdoor-rated lanterns provide modest table-level light; the view and changing daylight remain the terrace's main event.
