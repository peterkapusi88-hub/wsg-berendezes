# S13 · Westside Grand Continuum · STYLE IDENTITY SHEET V3.3

Státusz: kreatív terv; katalógus-, geometriai és szakági kapuk külön.

## DNS és színpaletta

The private continuation of the actual Westside Grand lobby: broad warm-grey timber planes, a dark mineral base, pale luminous frames and the measured rhythm of vertical divisions. The real lobby photographs provide a material grammar, while the apartment stays domestic and generous. COURTYARD MEASURE is the original signature: unequal rectangles and one circular void composed from the building's sheltered garden and lobby proportions, without copying the logo or turning the room into a reception desk.

The frame is an applied furniture detail with 6 cm-wide rails, a real shadow gap and a removable lower section for services. A 245 × 44 × 40 cm four-door graphite cabinet has a dark mineral-effect laminate top with restrained pale veining, a reference to the lobby's dark bases rather than solid marble. A 65 × 250 × 40 cm closed tower at the south end uses two broad wood-effect doors. Three fine vertical shadow joints sit above the tower, with deliberately unequal spacing; the remaining wall stays broad and calm. The TV is supported independently at the rear wall. Hidden light catches the inner pale frame on one side and leaves the rest in real shadow.
