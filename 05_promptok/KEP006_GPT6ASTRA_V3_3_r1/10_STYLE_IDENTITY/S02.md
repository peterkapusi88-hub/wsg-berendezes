# S02 · BiBo Nocturne · STYLE IDENTITY SHEET V3.3

Státusz: kreatív terv; katalógus-, geometriai és szakági kapuk külön.

## DNS és színpaletta

An intimate nocturnal salon shaped by navy cabinetry, a compact blue conversation basin and a single silver reflection. Large opaque blue planes meet silver-grey woven walls; plum enters only the private room. The mood is cultured and slightly theatrical, with precise rather than abundant metal.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

SILVER CUT: a 360 × 288 × 3.8 cm silver-grey textile-effect background supports an asymmetric navy composition: a 90 × 250 × 40 cm south cabinet and a 240 × 46 × 40 cm low cabinet, both matte navy laminate with white interiors. A fixed, safety-backed 24 × 190 cm silver mirror strip sits within the cabinet-side surround, with its lower edge 45 cm above the floor. The mirror has a softly irregular metal outline but a genuinely planar reflecting surface. A 190 cm clear rear-plane screen bay is held between blue framing planes; one narrow illuminated recess makes the blue appear deep without turning every joint into a light source.

## Integrált főmű

A broad plum-black crescent is cut by a thin silver-grey broken arc, spread across a full-height navy-grey wallcovering field. The darkest area sits below eye level; the upper third breathes into the wall colour. A short applied satin-metal edge catches light without a luminous outline around the entire work. This is a two-material architectural print with one removable edge, never an illuminated nightclub mural. W03, W04 and W07 retain the established style ground, actual grey doors and white window frames. Keep switches, thermostat, sensors and service points visible and operable. All panel dimensions follow surveyed wall extents, not image-derived dimensions.
