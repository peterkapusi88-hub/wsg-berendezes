# S04 · LA Horizon · STYLE IDENTITY SHEET V3.3

Státusz: kreatív terv; katalógus-, geometriai és szakági kapuk külön.

## DNS és színpaletta

Cinematic horizontal luxury: taupe seating, tobacco-toned transparent objects, long walnut-effect planes and cool silver-grey woven walls. The actual Buda panorama is the distant horizon, and furniture establishes a second, lower horizon inside the room.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

DOUBLE HORIZON: two floor-supported walnut-effect storage volumes, 180 × 38 × 38 cm and 120 × 62 × 38 cm, overlap visually within a 330 cm-wide composition but remain separate working cabinets. A 330 × 90 × 3 cm horizontal laminate panel floats visually above their tops on concealed wall cleats; a separate 65 × 220 × 38 cm south cabinet provides tall storage. The screen sits on the structural rear wall, surrounded by silver-grey weave. A 190 cm clear screen zone prevents the upper line crowding the television. All projecting surfaces have credible supports and honest manufactured edges.

## Integrált főmű

Carry one long tobacco-grey horizon across the usable W02 wall width, interrupted by a broad pale offset plane. Keep white wall visibly above and below this deliberately horizontal composition. Use woven wallcovering and a narrow walnut-effect applied band, no floating picture frame. Its line aligns visually with the dining furniture while respecting every control and return; the work need not become full-height to be architectural. W03, W04 and W07 retain the established style ground, actual grey doors and white window frames. Keep switches, thermostat, sensors and service points visible and operable. All panel dimensions follow surveyed wall extents, not image-derived dimensions.

## Konyha

Horizontal walnut-effect lower fronts, plain silver-grey uppers and a thin charcoal concrete-effect laminate worktop form long calm bands. Use the same material on the washable splashback with understated edge joints. The D13 refrigerator tower is silver-grey, bounded by a walnut-effect side panel; the south tall units repeat the horizontal handle datum.

## Bútorazonosság

## Kisasztal, textilek, tárgyak

Use taupe and tobacco cushions, a fine pearl-grey throw and a compact 80 × 32 × 40 cm low walnut-effect coffee slab on two inset support cheeks. A 30 × 45 × 30 cm side block holds a low smoke-glass vase. The brown wool rug stops clearly before the main walking paths; the existing floor remains visible as an independent material.

## Master és gardrób

A 285 × 105 × 6 cm low pearl-grey headboard is crossed by a broad tobacco textile band, both terminating cleanly before the electrical controls. Bedding is chalk and pearl, with one tobacco throw folded horizontally at the foot. The north six-door wardrobe uses walnut-effect lower front panels and pearl upper panels at one common horizontal division; the south strip repeats the division. The large storage wall reads as a quiet horizon, not extra architecture.

## Terasz

A charcoal aluminium outdoor two-seat sofa, target 160 × 80 cm, and two 70 × 75 cm chairs use ash-grey cushions and low, simple profiles. A 70 × 45 cm graphite outdoor table carries one pale serving bowl. A separate 140 × 80 cm charcoal outdoor dining table and four slim chairs occupy a measured lower paved bay. These budgeted procurement envelopes require product selection. Preserve the existing dark-brown WPC as an existing material rather than recolouring it to match new ash-grey furniture. Keep every step, the planted roof, railings and real city panorama. Two rechargeable outdoor-rated lanterns provide modest table-level light; the view and changing daylight remain the terrace's main event.
