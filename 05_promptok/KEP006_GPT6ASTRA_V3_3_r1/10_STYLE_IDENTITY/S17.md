# S17 · Mercedes Design Collection Residence · STYLE IDENTITY SHEET V3.3

Státusz: kreatív terv; katalógus-, geometriai és szakági kapuk külön.

## DNS és színpaletta

CONTINUOUS LINE connects five distinct domestic moods through oyster textile, cool grey timber, graphite bases and a fine satin champagne-silver line. The living room is composed from G-Class rounded squares, the kitchen-dining room from an AMG GT long tapered sweep, the master from S-Class soft layered comfort, room 05.2 from SL open arcs and 05.3 from C-Class precise compact divisions. Each room changes the line’s rhythm rather than changing the palette. This is an independent owner-inspired interior, with no official manufacturer affiliation, logos or copied dashboard.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

FIVE TEMPOS TV WALL: a low 245 × 43 × 40 cm oyster cabinet floats over a graphite plinth, with a broad 190 cm quiet screen field and an offset grey timber panel rising to 288 cm. A 65 × 245 × 40 cm grey timber storage tower sits at the south end. Two shallow rounded-square recesses on its fixed cheek, one open and one closed, provide the G-Class signature without grille bars. The TV mounts independently to the rear wall. A fine satin champagne-silver strip runs horizontally through the fixed panels and ends in a soft curve at the storage side; it does not cross moving doors or become an illuminated neon line. The composition is lighter, more horizontal and less armoured than S16.

## Integrált főmű

Its lower end makes a softly squared turn, linking the living-room G-Class form to the dining-room GT curve. A shallow champagne-silver applied segment catches grazing light at one point only. The remaining surface fades into the adjacent wall. Use textile print and a removable thin metal-effect edge; no model name is required for the motif to work. Keep controls, service access, original grey doors and white window frames intact; W07 is the quiet oyster ground.

## Kisasztal, textilek, tárgyak

## Master és gardrób

S-Class becomes a broad 270 × 155 × 7 cm oyster upholstered headboard with three overlapping horizontal cushions and one soft swept seam near the dry-side end. The fine champagne-silver fixed surround disappears at the upper edge so the headboard feels upholstered rather than framed. Keep the wall above unprinted. Full-height wardrobes are cool-grey timber-effect with oyster upper infills, real six-door north divisions and a discreet satin pull line. The south wardrobe repeats the same operating grid at its own measured width. Ivory bedding, layered oyster and grey-blue textiles and two small opal bedside lights create calm. The model signature is the gentle continuous stitched sweep, not an S-Class label.
