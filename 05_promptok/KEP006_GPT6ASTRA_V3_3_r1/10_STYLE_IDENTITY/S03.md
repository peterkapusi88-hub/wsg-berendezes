## DNS és színpaletta

A cultivated Mediterranean-Italian salon with olive seating, clay-coloured planes, warm grey linen and walnut-effect storage. Broad rounded edges and a small number of authentic ceramic objects give the room an artisan character without rustic architecture.

LIGHTING IDENTITY. At2700K evening white ambience, use a warm low cocoa cabinet wash and a short graze across the chalk textile return, exposing hand-finished texture. Keep2200–2700K dimmable Philips Hue ambience; no decorative colour wash. Master: a broad linen headboard rear wash, with functional wardrobe interiors and no glowing olive decorative stripe. The approximately3mconcealed Hue Lightstrip headboard route, two Hue Go bedside lamps and separately switched functional wardrobe interior light are retained. Kitchen task light is a continuous diffused under-upper-cabinet line. Bathroom light uses the style-specific mirror gesture and an independent continuous under-vanity wash. Every strip is hidden behind an aluminium/opal profile lip, with no visible LED dots or direct reflection in glass. New light is built into furniture or shallow removable panels, never a ceiling cove, lowered ceiling or invented spotlight. Existing points only: W01 D16, W02 D19, headboard D20, bedside D21/D23, wardrobe D26, kitchen D02 subject to verification; bathroom uses the existing basin/ceiling circuit only after wet-zone and permanent-supply approval. Each additional route needs its own correctly sized compatible kit, accessible ventilated driver and documented concealed cable path; do not assume an unapproved split, extension or new socket. Keep the original floor, tile and white ceiling readable at restrained mid-key exposure.
