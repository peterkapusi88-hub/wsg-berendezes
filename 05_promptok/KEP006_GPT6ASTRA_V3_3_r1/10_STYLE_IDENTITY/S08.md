## DNS és színpaletta

A collector's composition of unequal orthogonal bodies, chalk, graphite and one muted-yellow plane. The colour is part of the working cabinet geometry, not applied decoration; a measured imbalance creates energy while the grey-beige U anchors the room.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

RADICAL BRIDGE: a 120 × 58 × 38 cm graphite cabinet and a 120 × 38 × 38 cm chalk cabinet flank a supported 90 cm-wide joining shelf within a 350 cm overall composition. A 70 × 220 × 38 cm tall unit has one broad muted-yellow laminate face. The wall behind is chalk-textured panel, maximum 3.5 cm deep, with a 190 cm clear rear-wall screen area. The bridge is fixed to engineered side cheeks; it reads as an intentional void rather than a gravity-defying slab.

## Integrált főmű

A large chalk rectangle, a narrower graphite plane and one small muted-yellow slot form an off-centre full-height assembly. The yellow occupies less than a tenth of the field. Use flat printed laminate or textile panels with genuine joints and one shallow projecting edge. Preserve the radical TV bridge and wardrobe grid, while all secondary wall art is omitted. W03, W04 and W07 retain the established style ground, actual grey doors and white window frames. Keep switches, thermostat, sensors and service points visible and operable. All panel dimensions follow surveyed wall extents, not image-derived dimensions.

## Konyha

Chalk lower laminate fronts and graphite upper fronts use one muted-yellow refrigerator side panel as the punctuation. A grey concrete-effect laminate worktop and matching cleanable backsplash give neutral continuity. Handle lines follow actual modules; colour never creates false doors or an impossible extra cabinet.
