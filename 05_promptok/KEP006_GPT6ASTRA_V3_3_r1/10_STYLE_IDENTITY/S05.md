# S05 · Miami Streamline · STYLE IDENTITY SHEET V3.3

Státusz: kreatív terv; katalógus-, geometriai és szakági kapuk külön.

## DNS és színpaletta

Enduring Deco expressed through a few broad stepped contours, dusty-rose upholstery and champagne-grey planes. Rounded corners and graphic shadow lines provide glamour while charcoal edges keep the room precise; turquoise appears as a small textile note.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

SOFT STREAMLINE: three applied contour panels, 350 × 253 cm overall and maximum 4 cm deep, step back in wide unequal intervals around a 190 cm clear screen field. The panels are champagne-grey laminate faced MDF with radiused fixed corners, never painted architectural arches. A 240 × 48 × 38 cm low cabinet has four flat fronts and one curved fixed end; a 65 × 220 × 38 cm south cupboard repeats only the largest radius. One concealed upper line grazes the stepped panel, producing soft physical shadows.

## Integrált főmű

A chalk stepped field turns around a single smoky turquoise curve on W02; a narrow champagne edge recedes into the wall rather than closing a frame. Extend the composition from skirting to ceiling with large areas of quiet pearl ground. Use printed vlies and shallow fixed corner pieces, avoiding heavy sculpted joinery. The staircase motif is graphic, not a real step or altered ceiling. W03, W04 and W07 retain the established style ground, actual grey doors and white window frames. Keep switches, thermostat, sensors and service points visible and operable. All panel dimensions follow surveyed wall extents, not image-derived dimensions.

## Konyha

Champagne-grey flat laminate fronts with graphite recessed handle lines, a chalk-grey laminate worktop and a fine-grained matching splashback. One muted turquoise refrigerator side plane is the sole kitchen colour accent. All corners inside the galley remain straight and functional; the Deco rounding is limited to exposed furniture-end detailing.

## Kisasztal, textilek, tárgyak

Use two champagne cushions and one muted turquoise lumbar cushion against the dusty-rose corduroy. A 70 × 35 × 45 cm champagne laminate coffee block has one rounded end and a charcoal inset base; the small side block repeats that base. ## Master és gardrób

A 270 × 155 × 7 cm champagne textile headboard has two broad stepped padded panels with graphite piping, the highest segment deliberately off-centre. Chalk bedding and a pearl coverlet have one dusty-rose narrow border. Wardrobe fronts are flat pearl laminate with two large rounded-rectangle printed outlines spanning the six north doors; the south wardrobe has the same print scale and real working joints.

## Terasz

A charcoal aluminium outdoor two-seat sofa, target 160 × 80 cm, and two 70 × 75 cm chairs use ash-grey cushions and low, simple profiles. A 70 × 45 cm graphite outdoor table carries one pale serving bowl. A separate 140 × 80 cm charcoal outdoor dining table and four slim chairs occupy a measured lower paved bay. These budgeted procurement envelopes require product selection. Preserve the existing dark-brown WPC as an existing material rather than recolouring it to match new ash-grey furniture. Keep every step, the planted roof, railings and real city panorama. Two rechargeable outdoor-rated lanterns provide modest table-level light; the view and changing daylight remain the terrace's main event.
