## DNS és színpaletta

Enduring Deco expressed through a few broad stepped contours, dusty-rose upholstery and champagne-grey planes. Rounded corners and graphic shadow lines provide glamour while charcoal edges keep the room precise; turquoise appears as a small textile note.

## TV-fal / megtartott vagy új kompozíció

The panels are champagne-grey laminate faced MDF with radiused fixed corners, never painted architectural arches. A 240 × 48 × 38 cm low cabinet has four flat fronts and one curved fixed end; a 65 × 220 × 38 cm south cupboard repeats only the largest radius. One concealed upper line grazes the stepped panel, producing soft physical shadows. Keep the stepped champagne-grey TV silhouette but reduce it to three broad fixed planes, with only one rounded external corner and a thin graphite base. A small smoky-turquoise fixed inset appears below eye level. It is domestic Streamline architecture, not an Art Deco stage set; no chrome strip outlines every door.
